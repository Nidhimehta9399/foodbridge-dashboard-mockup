Dashboard — 5 KPI tiles + 6 report tabs

Sidebar + header app shell, five KPI tiles (Today · Yesterday · This Month · Last Month · All-Time Sales) over six report tabs — Product Sales · Recent Orders · Discount Report · Order Cycle · Salesman Route Report · Outstanding Recovery. Live in the prototype: tab switching, period + sort filters, order status filter and change, expandable order rows with line items, discount By Customer/By Route toggle with search, order-cycle legend filtering with derived cycle progress, route-report route/staff filters, and recovery filtering by cause, route or customer with expandable per-invoice ageing.

Report tabs — the question each one answers

Tab 1
Product Sales
Ranked products by sales or qty for a chosen period; article number + sale unit
 Tab 2
Recent Orders
Latest orders with status transitions, dispatch/delivery chips, invoice + timeline actions
 Tab 3
Discount Report
Order value → discount → net value, by customer or by route
 Tab 4
Order Cycle
Who is overdue against their own ordering cadence — the re-order prompt list
 Tab 5
Salesman Route Report
Cash vs UPI collected, opening cash, expense, handed over, and the difference to chase
 Tab 6
Outstanding Recovery
Where the cash is bleeding and why. One verdict naming the round that is ageing worst, three ranked actions, then the route league, the causes and the shops behind them

Reachable from Recent Orders

Order Timeline
Clock button → /order-timeline/<invoice>/<status>. Fulfilment steps derived from the status graph.
Order (detail)
Eye button → /order/<invoice>/<status>. Details grid, items, and both print flows.
