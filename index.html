Foodbridge Dashboard — Mockup v1

A static, clickable mockup of the admin dashboard module: five KPI tiles over five report tabs, plus the screens its row actions lead to. Plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JS driven by a seed file — no framework, no build step, no backend.

The five report tabs

Tabs switch in-page, so these all open the same screen — the description tells you what to click.

Product Sales

Tab 1

Products ranked by sales or quantity for a chosen period, with the article number and sale unit. Five periods, five sort orders, paginated at 8.

Recent Orders

Tab 2

Latest orders with dispatch/delivery chips. Expand a row for Details · Items · Comments · Fulfillment; hover the ⚡ for Smart Insights; Invoice ▾ offers A4 and Thermal printing.

Discount Report

Tab 3

Order value → discount → net, by customer or by route. Rows expand into their products; By Route swaps in a Round Off column and per-page totals.

Order Cycle

Tab 4

Who is overdue against their own ordering cadence — the re-order prompt list. Filter by Overdue · Due Soon · On Track · New.

Salesman Route Report

Tab 5

Cash vs UPI collected, opening cash, expense, handed over — and the difference to chase, red when the salesman is short.

How to read this

  • • It is a mockup, not the product. Nothing is saved — every interaction is local to the page, and a reload resets it. There is no backend.
  • • The data is invented. Representative shapes and volumes, not real figures.
  • • A few edges are deliberate dead ends, and say so when clicked: creating an order, sending a reminder, and connecting a thermal printer all belong to other modules.
  • • Frozen at v1. Everything under /v1/ is the accepted snapshot, unaltered. Work in progress lives under /discovery/ and is not yet accepted. Later iterations get their own version folder; this one will not change.
  • • Needs a network connection — the stylesheet loads from a CDN at runtime.
